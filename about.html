Our Story

About La Maison

A journey of culinary passion that spans decades, cultures, and countless unforgettable meals.

A Legacy of Excellence

Founded in 1998, La Maison was born from a simple belief: that dining is more than sustenance — it is an art form. Our founder, Chef Antoine Laurent, envisioned a place where culinary mastery meets warm hospitality.

Over the past two decades, we have grown from a small bistro into one of the region's most celebrated fine dining destinations. Yet our philosophy remains unchanged: source the finest ingredients, honor traditional techniques, and innovate with purpose.

Today, our team of award-winning chefs continues to push boundaries while staying true to the soul of classic cuisine. Every dish that leaves our kitchen is a testament to our commitment to excellence.

Our Journey

1998

The Beginning

Chef Antoine Laurent opens La Maison as a modest 30-seat bistro in the heart of the culinary district.

2005

First Michelin Star

La Maison receives its first Michelin star, recognizing our commitment to culinary excellence.

2012

Expansion

We expand to three locations, bringing the La Maison experience to more discerning diners.

2020

Modern Reinvention

A complete renovation transforms our flagship restaurant, blending timeless elegance with contemporary design.

2025

Continuing the Legacy

With three Michelin stars and countless awards, we continue our pursuit of culinary perfection every single day.
